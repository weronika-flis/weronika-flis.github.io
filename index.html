Research Interests

Cranial Sexual Dimorphism & Facial Geometry

Investigating 3D geometric morphometrics and anthropometric facial variation in contemporary European populations to establish refined sex estimation criteria.

3D & Digital Facial Approximation

Virtual skull preparation, anatomical muscle placement, and tissue depth landmarking using SensAble Freeform Plus, Pixologic ZBrush, and Unreal Engine.

Forensic Osteology & Bioarchaeology

Conducting in-situ skeletal recovery, anatomical-anthropological analyses, taphonomic assessments, and age/sex determinations from archaeological and modern collections.

Developmental Asymmetry & Phenotypic Markers

Examining fluctuating asymmetry and digit ratio (2D:4D) as biological indicators of developmental instability and early-life environmental exposures.

Doctoral School of Exact and Natural Sciences · Faculty of Biology, Jagiellonian University, Kraków
